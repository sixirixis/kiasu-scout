The KiasuScout flywheel

Parents get better discovery

  • Ask natural-language questions by child age, location, goal and budget.
  • Compare providers with transparent fit reasons.
  • Give lightweight feedback: saved, contacted, too expensive, too far, not enough info.

Businesses get better AEO intelligence

  • See where AI assistants mention or miss them.
  • Learn what parents actually ask and reject.
  • Turn feedback into content, reviews, schema and profile fixes.
Parent questions → recommendations → feedback → AEO insights → better business profiles → better parent recommendations
